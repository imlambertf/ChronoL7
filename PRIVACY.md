# Privacy Policy for ChronoLab - Desktop Calendar & Notes

**Last Updated:** November 2026  
**Publisher:** Lambert (imlambertf)

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
**ChronoLab - Desktop Calendar & Notes** operates 100% offline as a standalone desktop utility. We do **not** collect, transmit, rent, or share any personal information, usage telemetry, diagnostics, or calendar contents to any external servers or third parties.

## 2. Local Storage
All user-created notes, reminders, holiday configurations, and window layout preferences are stored strictly locally on your device inside your Windows user profile folder (%APPDATA%\CalendarWidget).

## 3. User Control and Deletion
You have complete control over your data at all times:
- You can view, edit, back up (.zip), or delete your notes directly within the application.
- Uninstalling the application or deleting the %APPDATA%\CalendarWidget directory permanently removes all stored data from your device.
